About Nakiah's Closet

Modest wear for women, designed and sold from Jinja, Uganda.

Our story

Nakiah's Closet started as a small tailoring effort for friends and family who wanted abayas and deras that were properly fitted, comfortable in Uganda's climate, and still beautiful enough to wear on Eid or to a wedding. That word-of-mouth business has grown into a closet that now serves women across Jinja and beyond.

Every abaya, dera and veil we sell is chosen with the same questions in mind: does it cover well, does it move well, and will she want to wear it again next week?

We sell exclusively to women, and we handle every order ourselves — from the first WhatsApp message to the day it's delivered to your door.

What guides us

  • 1

    Modesty first

    Every piece is cut generously and lined properly, so coverage is never an afterthought.

  • 2

    Made to be worn

    We choose fabrics that hold up to daily life in Uganda's heat, not just photographs.

  • 3

    A woman-to-woman shop

    We sell only to women, and every conversation about sizing, fit or delivery is handled directly by our team.
